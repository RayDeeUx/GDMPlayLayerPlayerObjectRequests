# HarkenPlatformerInputsEverywhere
Re-listen to Platformer inputs on Classic levels like you did before 2.209 released.

Based on (and adapted from) Windows and Android decompilations by chagh.

Useful for levels such as [Pirdal Platformy](level:133757547).

This [Geode mod](https://geode-sdk.org) is licensed under LGPLv2. To report bugs or get help with using my mods, press the Discord button in the lower left corner.
